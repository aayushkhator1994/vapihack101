Every time I closed my laptop, my work stopped.

So I built Laptop Closed: shut the lid and your phone rings with where you left off. Keep shipping by voice while the work runs in the cloud.

Built in 90 min at the @Vapi_AI × Build Club hackathon 🧵

How it works:
→ Close the lid, phone rings: "You were on branch main, last commit was…"
→ Ask a question, it researches live and answers
→ Ask for a build, it hands it off and calls you back when it's done

The part I like most: it doesn't ping you for everything.

When a task finishes, Jev scores it: call you, send a quiet push, or stay silent. A finished landing page gets a call. A failed run stays quiet.
