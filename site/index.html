How it works, step by step

Each step lists exactly which tool does what. The colors match the table and cards below.

1

You close the lid

  • Our serverChecks the Mac's lid sensor every second. The Mac stays awake with the lid shut, so the server keeps running.
2

It snapshots where you left off

  • Our serverReads your git branch, last three commits and the top item in TODO.md.
3

Your phone buzzes and rings

  • VapiPlaces an outbound phone call from your Vapi number, opening with the snapshot.
  • DeepgramAura 2 speaks the opening line in the agent's voice.
  • ntfySends a push notification. Tapping it opens the same agent as a browser call (Vapi web SDK).

"Laptop closed. You were on branch main. Your last commits were… Want me to keep going?"

4

You talk to the agent

  • DeepgramNova 3 turns your speech into text; Aura 2 speaks every reply.
  • VapiRuns the conversation (turn-taking, interruptions, noise filtering) and, when the agent needs a tool, sends the request to our server.
  • JevClassifies each request as research, build or status, and logs the decision on the dashboard.

5

The build runs in the cloud

  • ManusBuilds and deploys the page on its own.

6

  • JevReads the finished result and picks one: call you, send a quiet push, or stay silent.
7

  • VapiCalls your phone again with the summary.

  • ntfyPushes a notification that links straight to what Manus built.

Each tool's job

Every sponsor has a clear role, and Vapi carries the most.

Vapi

The voice agent, the phone line and the conversation engine.

  • Hosts the agent: the "Laptop Closed" assistant with its prompt, GPT-4o model, Deepgram voice, noise settings and four tools.
  • Calls you: the outbound /call API rings your phone when the lid closes and when a task finishes.
  • Answers you: your Vapi number is attached to the agent, so calling it reaches the same assistant.
  • Runs the conversation: turn-taking, interruptions and noise filtering, then sends tool requests to our server and speaks the answers.
  • Browser call: the push opens a page that starts the agent through Vapi's web SDK.
Steps 3, 4, 7 · setup_vapi.py, /vapi/tools, /talk

Deepgram

The agent's ears and voice.

  • Nova 3 transcribes your speech in real time.
  • Aura 2 (Thalia voice) speaks every reply.
Steps 3, 4, 7 · Vapi transcriber + voice

Exa

Live research with sources.

  • Answer API returns a cited answer, trimmed to two spoken sentences.
  • Search API is the fallback if the answer call fails.
Step 4 · research tool

Manus

Long work in the cloud.

  • Creates tasks from your spoken request, like "build a landing page".
  • Builds and deploys while your laptop is shut.
Steps 4, 5 · delegate tool

Jev

Decisions: what you asked for, and whether to interrupt you.

  • Routes each request: research, build or status.
  • Scores finished tasks: call, push only or silent.
Steps 4, 6 · TypeSafe System One

ntfy

Push notifications.

  • Alerts your phone on lid close and task finish.
  • Tap opens the voice call or the Manus result.
Steps 3, 7

Our server

The glue.

  • Watches the lid and takes the snapshot.
  • Handles tool calls, tracks tasks and serves the live dashboard.
